# DesignVerse 2027 - Speaker Registration

Welcome to DesignVerse 2027. Thank you for considering becoming a speaker, we appreciate your efforts to help inspire our participants! DesignVerse 2027 is a designathon spanning 24 hours on May 16, 2027.

## **Event Information**

**DesignVerse 2027** is a designathon spanning 24 hours through **April 17-18, 2027.**

Speakers will have the opportunity to present their expertise and insights to our participants during the event. This is a great opportunity to share knowledge and inspire the next generation of designers.

Note: Speakers are not permitted to become participants for the designathon.
